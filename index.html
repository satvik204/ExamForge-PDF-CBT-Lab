How to convert correctly

1. Upload the paper. 2. Click Auto-detect & crop all questions. 3. Inspect the crops flagged for review, and manually recrop any incomplete or misdetected questions. 4. Enter the answer key. 5. Start CBT and test zoom/marks. 6. Export EF1 code if you want to reuse it in ExamForge.

Image crops preserve mathematical notation and diagrams. Detection is automatic on text-selectable PDFs; image-only scanned PDFs and page-spanning questions may need manual correction. The app never guesses correct answers.
